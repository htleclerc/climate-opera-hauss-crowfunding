II · Fire — The Test

A boy, a talking bird,
and a sacred spring

Abdul, a young boy from Al Khobar who loves books, is called by a mysterious Talking Bird to cross the desert in search of the legendary Golden Fountain — a sacred spring said to bring prosperity to the land.

Drawing on Arabian folklore and contemporary environmental themes — water scarcity, education, cultural heritage and shared responsibility for the Earth — the opera unites classical opera with Arabic musical colours and choral writing.

Performed in English with Arabic influences and subtitles. Following the world premiere of Soria Moria, the two works form a diptych exploring two extremes of our changing planet: ice and desert.
