The libretto

Abdul, a young boy from Al Khobar who loves books, is called by a mysterious Talking Bird to cross the desert in search of the legendary Golden Fountain — a sacred spring said to bring prosperity to the land.

Drawing on Arabian folklore and contemporary environmental themes — water scarcity, education, cultural heritage and shared responsibility for the Earth — the opera joins classical opera with Arabic musical colours and choral writing.

Sung in English with Arabic influences and subtitles. With Soria Moria, it forms a diptych on two extremes of our changing planet: ice and desert.
